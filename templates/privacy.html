Privacy Policy

Jack and the Beanstalk Storybook is a student project. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics, when you sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile picture, and Google's account ID. We never see your Google password.
  • The photo you upload and the About Me details you type in (name, age, favorites, and so on).
  • The pictures the app draws for your book.

How we use it

  • To make your storybook. Your photo is sent to OpenAI's image service to create the cartoon character, and the character is sent to draw the pages.
  • To save your books to your account so you can come back to them.
  • To keep the service running: we log events such as sign-ins and whether each picture succeeded, identified by your Google account ID, not your email.

We don't sell your information, show ads, or share it with anyone other than the services needed to run the app (Google for sign-in, OpenAI for drawing, Amazon Web Services for storage, and Render for hosting).

Where it's stored

Your books, photo and pictures are stored privately in Amazon Web Services (AWS). Only you can see your books when you're signed in.

Deleting your data

Open a book and click Delete to permanently remove that book, its photo and its pictures. To remove everything, including your account record, email reuvikupchik@gmail.com.

Photos of other people

Only upload photos of yourself, or of people who have agreed (for a child, with a parent's permission).

Contact

Questions: reuvikupchik@gmail.com
